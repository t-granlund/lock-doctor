04 · What we built

Spruce Grove OS.

An agentic coding harness that lives on your machine and builds the small software your shop actually needs — then keeps maintaining it.

Runs on your machine

macOS, Linux, or WSL. Your files, your shell, your keys, local-first. There is no third-party pile of your customers.

Any brain, one line

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, a private endpoint — swapped in config. Models are rented. The harness is owned.

Nothing runs without your yes

Permission gates; approval before anything consequential. The harness makes care easy to do and hard to skip.

Open by birth

Apache-2.0 license. Built from and import-compatible with Code Puppy (Michael Pfaffenberger) — provenance in writing, PROVENANCE.md.

The computer doesn’t even need to be opened. An active internet connection and your voice — and it builds what you want to see and do, while you get to see the world.

07 · The mission

    
The misfits. The rebels.
The kind. The pure.

    

      
WHAT
The Leather Apron Club — the Junto, anno 1727, reborn in Bentonville. A circle that appoints builders and hands real tools to real tradespeople. The Tenant Forge and Hub exist to outsource and give away. Places like the 43 Institute fund the explorers; we build so they have time to explore.

      
WHY
Because the moment is severe. We developed speed and shut ourselves inside it — knowledge made us cynical, cleverness hard and unkind. Chaplin said it in 1940: “More than machinery, we need humanity. More than cleverness, we need kindness and gentleness.” Speed without either is just a faster lockout.

      
HOW
We appoint the kind, the pure, the misfits and rebels — then build beside them, train them as needed, and hand over the keys. All local. Full control. Nothing consequential runs without a human yes.

    

    
“How can the Junto assist you in any of your honorable designs?” — Franklin’s standing question. Reborn in Bentonville. You’ve been answering it since before we asked.
